FICTION, WITH PHYSICS

An imagined world.
A measurable sky.

The stellar masses come from measurements of Alpha Centauri. The compact orbits, planet, slow rotation, atmosphere, and terrain are imagined. This is an interpretation of the novel’s premise, rather than a reconstruction of its world or the real Alpha Centauri system.

Three stars interact through Newtonian gravity. The planet feels all three, with its own gravitational influence neglected. Surface sunlight follows their recorded positions. The landscape and enlarged stellar disks are artistic; incident light is an inverse-square measurement, not a temperature or climate prediction.

How the observation is made

The C++ solver uses AU, solar masses, and Julian years, with a fixed 1/16384-year leapfrog step. The browser interpolates a 36-year recording. Camera controls and playback speed change the view; they do not change the integrated trajectory. Stellar conservation checks exclude the massless planet.

Sources: Akeson et al. (2021) and Faria et al. (2022). See the model and reproducibility notes.
